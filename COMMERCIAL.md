# Commercial Use

## Public demonstrator vs. commercial IBRAMIND

IBRAMIND BidBox Agent is an open challenge demonstrator for bounded, auditable tender-compliance workflows using synthetic/open data.

Commercial IBRAMIND engagements may include:

- Tender and requirement extraction
- BOQ/document intelligence
- Compliance matrices and evidence traceability
- Organization-specific procurement rules
- Approval workflows and audit evidence
- Private institutional connectors
- Enterprise authentication and access control
- Governed agents with human approval boundaries
- Private deployment, integration, and support

## Paid pilot

A paid pilot can be scoped around a defined tender workflow, document set, evaluation process, and acceptance criteria.

Start at **https://ibramind.com** or contact **founder@ibramind.com**.

## Decision boundary

Commercial implementations remain decision-support systems. Final procurement decisions and approvals remain with authorized human reviewers and the responsible organization.
